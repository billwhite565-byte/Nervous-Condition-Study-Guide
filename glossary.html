Key Terms Glossary

Colonialism
A system in which one country controls another place politically, economically, and culturally.
Patriarchy
A social system in which men hold most authority and women are expected to have less power or freedom.
Assimilation
The pressure to adopt the language, values, or behavior of a dominant culture, sometimes at the cost of one's own identity.
Alienation
A feeling of separation from family, culture, community, or even oneself.
Mission School
A school connected to Christian missionary work; in the novel, it offers opportunity while also carrying colonial values.
Gender Inequality
Unequal treatment or expectations based on gender, especially the limits placed on girls and women in the novel.
Psychological Conflict
Inner struggle caused by competing pressures, desires, identities, or social expectations.
Critical Consciousness
The ability to recognize and question the systems of power shaping one's life.
